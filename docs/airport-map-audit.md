# Airport map verification — 22 October 2026

Reviewed all twelve official AIP documents served by the MACL AIP index. Chart positions were digitised relative to the physical runway, not treated as surveyed coordinates. OpenStreetMap terminal footprints were cross-checked for Kulhudhuffushi and Hoarafushi. An Overpass request failed; direct public OSM map records were obtained instead.

Unverified fixed facilities are omitted from the map rather than assigned template coordinates. Radio frequencies in chart headers do not establish tower location. Numbered incident-response pins remain exercise proposals. The Generic airport remains an explicitly illustrative template.

Corrections include Hoarafushi runway 15/33; Gan physical runway 2960 m (starter extensions and declared distances are separate); Dharavandhoo 1200 m; terminal/apron side corrections for Hanimaadhoo, Kadhdhoo, Maafaru and Hoarafushi. Velana includes the opposite-side ATC tower and both charted fire stations.
